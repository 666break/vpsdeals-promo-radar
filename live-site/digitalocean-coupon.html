Frequently asked questions

Is there an official DigitalOcean coupon code?

Not a single standing code on its public pricing or home pages. DigitalOcean's official Promotional Credit & Discount Terms show that discounts are delivered as a unique code tied to a specific offer (for example, a new-account promotion). The code is redeemed on the DigitalOcean site and shows its own amount and expiration when you receive it. We do not publish a specific code here because none is stated on an official public page we could open on 2026-10-14.

How do I get a DigitalOcean discount?

Two verified levers: (1) a promotional credit or discount code issued through a specific offer, redeemed on the DigitalOcean site (amount and expiry are shown in that offer); and (2) per-second billing, in effect since January 1, 2026, so short-lived or paused workloads are billed only for the seconds they run. The cheapest published plan is the Basic Droplet at $4.00/month.

What is the cheapest DigitalOcean Droplet in 2026?

The cheapest Basic Droplet is $4.00/month: 512 MiB RAM, 1 vCPU, 10 GiB SSD, and 500 GiB transfer. The next step up is 1 GiB RAM, 1 vCPU, 25 GiB SSD, 1,000 GiB transfer at $6.00/month.

Does DigitalOcean offer a refund?

DigitalOcean's Terms of Service state that any and all fees paid are non-refundable, except as otherwise set forth in the agreement or expressly agreed between the parties. Promotional credits are explicitly non-refundable. You may cancel your account at any time to avoid future charges, but that is not a refund of fees already paid.

Is there free shipping with DigitalOcean?

Not applicable. DigitalOcean sells cloud and digital services only (Droplets, storage, databases, networking) and ships no physical goods, so there is no shipping cost or free-shipping threshold. This is inferred from the official pricing page, which lists only digital services.

Where are DigitalOcean data centers?

DigitalOcean states it operates 20 data centers across 12 regions. Region choice matters for latency, so pick the data center physically closest to you or your users when you create a Droplet.
