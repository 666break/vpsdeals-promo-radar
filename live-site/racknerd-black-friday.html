What RackNerd Black Friday Actually Is

RackNerd is a budget KVM VPS specialist (20 datacenters, RAID10 SSD, full root, instant setup). Its Black Friday event is the annual moment when it pushes its KVM VPS promos the hardest — usually a set of yearly KVM plans at the lowest prices of the year, spanning the small entry box up through several larger tiers. Because RackNerd already runs a standing $2.24/mo KVM promo year-round, Black Friday is less of a "never-seen-before discount" and more of a "slightly deeper annual rate plus a few extra tiers." That context matters: a lot of "RackNerd Black Friday" content implies you're missing out if you don't wait, when in reality the standing promo is already near the floor.

Mechanically, the BF deal is identical to the standing promo in structure: KVM virtualization, RAID10 SSD, full root access, 20 locations, and an annual-prepay requirement to unlock the low price. The difference is the number and the depth of the discounted tiers available during the event window.
