Frequently asked questions

Is there an official Hostinger coupon code?

Yes. Hostinger's official coupon page displays the code COUPONSPAGE, which is applied automatically when you use the deals shown on that page. One code may be used per order, and existing customers can apply a code to a new plan.

How do I get a Hostinger discount?

Open Hostinger's official coupons or pricing page, choose a plan on a longer prepaid term (the largest advertised discounts appear on 48-month terms), and the discount is applied at checkout. The code COUPONSPAGE applies automatically, so you usually don't need to type it manually.

How much can I save with Hostinger?

Official discounts shown on Hostinger's coupon page include up to 76% off Premium, 80% off Unlimited, and 73% off Cloud Startup hosting on 48-month prepaid terms. Hostinger's FAQ also mentions up to 90% off on certain hosting, VPS, or AI Builder plans. Renewal is at the standard (higher) monthly rate.

Does Hostinger offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes — a 30-day money-back guarantee. A refund is available if you cancel within 30 days of the transaction. Hosting plans are refundable except the first payment after a Free Trial and all VPS plans; domain names are refundable only within 96 hours of registration. Crypto payments are non-refundable. The refund policy was last revised on 2026-09-08.

Is there free shipping or a free domain?

Hostinger is a digital product, so there is no free shipping. A free domain for one year is included when you purchase a yearly-or-longer hosting plan through Hostinger's official order page.
