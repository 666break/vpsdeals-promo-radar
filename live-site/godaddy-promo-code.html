GoDaddy's US-EN official offers page does not currently display an alphanumeric promo code. The running headline deals (.com for $0.01 with a 3-year term, .com for $4.99 with a 1-year term, up to 50% off SSL, up to 46% off Website Security) are auto-applied at checkout.

Does GoDaddy offer a money-back guarantee?

GoDaddy offers a 30-day (720-hour) refund window on hosting services that have not yet been performed. Domain refund windows depend on the type: 5 days for new registrations, 5 days for manual renewals, 45 days for 1-year auto-renewals, and 5 days for multi-year auto-renewals. Premium domains and several specific TLDs are non-refundable.

Are GoDaddy domains refundable?

Standard new and renewal registrations follow the windows above. Premium domain names, Domain Lock / Domain Reservation, certain TLDs (e.g. .AI, .NU, .AM, .AT), transfer fees on successful transfers, cloud-server purchases, merchant accounts, paid support once performed, and Office Max packages are explicitly non-refundable.
