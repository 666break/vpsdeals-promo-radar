Company background (verified)

Contabo is a German limited company (Contabo GmbH) headquartered at Welfenstrasse 22, 81541 Munich, Germany. Its about page states it was founded in spring 2003 as Giga-International and renamed Contabo in early 2013 as it internationalised. It is registered at AG Munich (HRB 180722) with VAT ID DE267602842; managing directors are listed as Stephan Wolfram and Mario Wilhelm. The company reports 275,000+ customers in 190 countries, around 300 staff, and 450,000+ servers, and marks "23 years in business." This is a long-running, real operator — not a coupon-site shell.

Storage VPS and Windows VPS lines (verified)

One reason Contabo is worth a look is that it sells purpose-built product lines, not just one VPS shape. Confirmed on contabo.com:

  • Storage VPS — a line tuned for large disk capacity (backups, media, file sync) rather than CPU, useful when you need space more than compute.
  • Windows VPS — VPS with Windows Server images for workloads that require Windows (e.g., some trading platforms, .NET apps).
  • Cloud VDS — virtual dedicated servers with dedicated vCPUs (no oversell), for when shared cores are a risk you can't take.
  • cPanel VPS, dedicated servers, and GPU solutions round out the catalog.

Where Contabo runs (verified locations)

Contabo operates 9 regions and 11 locations across 4 continents. Locations span Europe (including its Munich and Nuremberg data centers), the United Kingdom, the United States (central, east, west), Singapore, Japan, Australia, and India. Every plan includes a free firewall and DDoS protection, dedicated IPv4/IPv6, and a customer control panel. For a VPS, region choice drives latency — pick the location closest to your users.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very high RAM and vCPU per dollar (Cloud VPS 4: 4 vCPU / 8 GB at $5.28–$6.60/mo).
  • Real, long-running company: GmbH, Munich, since 2003, 275K+ customers.
  • Purpose-built lines: Storage VPS, Windows VPS, Cloud VDS (dedicated vCPU).
  • Broad global footprint: 9 regions / 11 locations / 4 continents.
  • Free firewall + DDoS protection, dedicated IPv4/IPv6, 99.9% uptime.
  • Generous unlimited traffic (fair-use) on Cloud VPS.

Cons

  • Entry Cloud VPS uses shared vCPUs — oversell risk under neighbor load.
  • The $5.28 rate is a 24-month intro, not month-to-month.
  • 200 Mbit/s port on entry Cloud VPS (1 Gbit/s only on higher tiers / VDS).
  • Unmanaged — you harden and maintain the server yourself.
  • Support is not marketed as 24/7 in-house the way some competitors claim.

Who should pick Contabo

Contabo fits budget-conscious builders who want maximum RAM/vCPU per dollar and don't mind shared cores: a personal project, a staging box, a backup/media host (Storage VPS), or a Windows workload (Windows VPS). The wide location list makes regional placement easy, and the 23-year operating history means it isn't going to vanish overnight. If you can commit to the 24-month term, Cloud VPS 4 at $5.28/mo is one of the better raw-spec deals on the market.

Who should look elsewhere

Skip entry Cloud VPS if you need dedicated, predictable CPU — the shared vCPU model can slow under neighbor load, and you should step up to Cloud VDS. Also skip it if you need true month-to-month flexibility, since the best price is term-locked. For transparent per-second billing and a huge global footprint, compare DigitalOcean (Basic Droplet from $4.00/mo); for a flat beginner-friendly price, Hostinger (KVM 1 at $6.49/mo on a 2-year term).
