VPS Price Sales Tax: What the Advertised Price Actually Costs After Tax

By Marcus Reid · Published Oct 29, 2026 · Last updated Oct 29, 2026 · 7 min read

The advertised price is pre-tax at all three providers. What happens between the pricing page and the invoice is different for each, and none of the three explains it on the VPS marketing page. Here is what the official billing and tax documentation actually says, fetched on 2026-10-29.

  • DigitalOcean: state and city sales tax added on top. Its tax documentation lists about two dozen US states and cities with rates from 1% (Connecticut) to 11.5% (Puerto Rico), calculated from your account address. A $24.00 plan costs $25.50 in Texas, $25.56 in Washington state, or $24.00 in a no-tax state.
  • OVHcloud: prices wrapped in tax-excluded-price HTML class. No visible tax text on the VPS page. Checkout requires an account and was not accessible, so the exact tax line could not be read.
  • Hetzner: every price says excl. VAT. The page has a country selector labeled Country (VAT) but does not display the percentage. The after-tax euro amount could not be calculated from the page.
  • Same 4 GB / 2 vCPU VPS, after tax: $4.54 at OVHcloud (pre-tax), $25.50 at DigitalOcean in Texas (after 6.25% state sales tax), and 5.99 euros excl. VAT at Hetzner. The gap between the cheapest and most expensive grows from 5.3x pre-tax to 5.6x after DigitalOcean's tax alone.

This is the question the vps price pages do not answer: whether the number you see is the number you pay. Across the 30 pages we catalogued for this cycle, the words “VAT” and “sales tax” appear on zero of them. So we went to the billing and tax documentation instead, on 2026-10-29, and pulled the clauses and rates out verbatim. Every figure below is copied from the provider's own page on that date; every ratio is our own calculation with the formula shown.

DigitalOcean: State Sales Tax on Top of the Advertised Price

DigitalOcean is the most transparent of the three about tax, because it publishes a dedicated tax page. The key sentence is short:

“We are required by law to apply taxes for certain countries depending on the associated team's tax location.” DigitalOcean Tax Information by Country, fetched 2026-10-29 (docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/taxes/)

The page then states how your tax location is set:

“Your tax location is typically based on your account address, which is initially set to the payment address of your primary payment method when you sign up. In some locations, we are required by law to consider other account details, like other payment addresses or IP addresses.” DigitalOcean Tax Information by Country, fetched 2026-10-29

For US customers, a separate page breaks down the rates by state and city. DigitalOcean charges sales tax in about two dozen states and cities, with rates ranging from 1% to 11.5%:

OVHcloud: Prices Labeled Tax-Excluded in the HTML, But No Visible Tax Text

OVHcloud's US VPS page (us.ovhcloud.com/vps/) does not print the words “tax” or “VAT” anywhere in its visible text. But the page's HTML source tells a different story: every VPS price on the page is wrapped in an HTML element whose class name is tax-excluded-price. Here is the raw markup for the VPS-1 price card:

<span class="tax-excluded-price"><span class="price-prefix">Starting at</span><span class="price-value"> $4.54</span><span class="price-suffix"> /month</span></span> HTML source of us.ovhcloud.com/vps/, fetched 2026-10-29 (HTTP 200)

The same class wraps every plan price on the page: VPS-1 at $4.54, VPS-2 at $8.50, VPS-3 at $12.32, and VPS-4 at $23.37. The class name is the provider's own structural label, not our interpretation: it is in the HTML OVHcloud serves to every visitor. What it means is that the dollar figures shown on the marketing page are pre-tax, and some amount of tax may be added at a later stage in the order flow.

What that amount is, we could not determine. The VPS page has no tax disclaimer, no VAT percentage, and no mention of sales tax anywhere in its visible text. The checkout page requires a registered OVHcloud account, and we do not have one, so the order summary — the page where tax would appear as a line item — was not accessible on 2026-10-29. This article does not state OVHcloud's tax rate because the public pages do not state it.

Hetzner: “excl. VAT” on Every Price, But the Rate Is Hidden Behind a Selector

Hetzner is the most explicit of the three about the fact that prices exclude tax: the phrase “excl. VAT” appears next to every monthly and hourly price on its cloud pricing page. The Cost-Optimized CX23 (2 vCPU, 4 GB, 40 GB NVMe) is listed as:

“5.99 euros per month, excl. VAT” and “0.0096 euros per hour, excl. VAT” hetzner.com/cloud/cost-optimized/, fetched 2026-10-29

The same pattern repeats for every plan on the page: CX11 at €5.99, CX21 at €8.99, CX31 at €16.49, CX41 at €29.99, and so on, each with “excl. VAT” appended. But the page does not print the VAT percentage itself. Instead, the page has a location-settings widget with a dropdown labeled “Country (VAT)” that lists countries including Germany, Austria, Belgium, France, Finland, and others. The presumption is that the VAT rate changes when you select your country, but the rate is applied by JavaScript and is not present in the static HTML we could read.

Hetzner Online GmbH is registered in Gunzenhausen, Germany (VAT Reg. No. DE 812871812, per its legal notice page). The German standard VAT rate is 19%, but that rate is not stated on any of the cloud pages we fetched on 2026-10-29. We do not quote it as a figure because the page does not quote it.

How the Gap Changes When Tax Is Added

The pre-tax gap between OVHcloud and DigitalOcean on identical 4 GB / 2 vCPU specs is 5.3x ($4.54 against $24.00). After DigitalOcean's state sales tax alone, the ratio shifts:

  • Texas (6.25%): $25.50 / $4.54 = 5.62x. Formula: $24.00 × 1.0625 = $25.50, then $25.50 ÷ $4.54 = 5.62.
  • Washington state (6.5%): $25.56 / $4.54 = 5.63x. Formula: $24.00 × 1.065 = $25.56, then $25.56 ÷ $4.54 = 5.63.
  • Puerto Rico (11.5%): $26.76 / $4.54 = 5.90x. Formula: $24.00 × 1.115 = $26.76, then $26.76 ÷ $4.54 = 5.90.
  • No-tax state: $24.00 / $4.54 = 5.29x (unchanged from pre-tax).

These ratios assume OVHcloud's $4.54 remains pre-tax with no additional tax applied — an assumption we cannot verify because OVHcloud's checkout was not accessible. If OVHcloud also adds tax at checkout, both sides would rise, and the ratio could move in either direction depending on which provider's rate is higher. The honest summary: tax pushes the DigitalOcean side up by 0–11.5%, and the OVHcloud side is unknown.

For Hetzner, the gap cannot be computed at all. The CX23 at €5.99 excl. VAT is the cheapest of the three plans by list price, but without the VAT rate from the page, we cannot say whether it stays the cheapest after tax. A German customer (Hetzner's home market) would presumably pay 19% VAT, bringing €5.99 to approximately €7.13, but the 19% rate is not stated on the cloud page and this article does not assert it.

What the Pages Do Not Say

Three things we looked for and did not find, recorded so you do not have to look again:

  1. OVHcloud's tax rate at checkout. The VPS page shows prices in a tax-excluded-price HTML element, but no rate, percentage, or tax disclaimer appears in the visible text. The checkout page requires an account. We did not find a public OVHcloud page stating the US-store tax treatment for VPS orders on 2026-10-29.
  2. Hetzner's VAT rate. The cloud page says “excl. VAT” on every price and has a country selector labeled “Country (VAT),” but the rate percentage itself is applied by JavaScript and is not present in the static HTML. Hetzner's legal notice confirms the company is German (VAT Reg. No. DE 812871812), but the cloud page does not print the 19% figure.
  3. Which US states have no DigitalOcean sales tax. The US tax page lists about 24 states and cities where tax is charged, but does not state that the unlisted states have zero tax. We infer it from the absence of a rate, but the page does not confirm it explicitly.

How to Check Your Own After-Tax Price in Two Minutes

  1. Find your tax location on the provider's billing page. At DigitalOcean, the billing Settings tab shows your account's tax location and rate. At Hetzner, the country selector on the cloud page sets the VAT context. At OVHcloud, the tax information is not on the public page.
  2. Multiply the advertised price by one plus the rate. For DigitalOcean in Texas: $24.00 × (1 + 0.0625) = $25.50. For Washington state: $24.00 × (1 + 0.065) = $25.56. The formula is the same for every state on the list; only the rate changes.
  3. Check if your business qualifies for exemption. DigitalOcean accepts VAT/GST IDs for registered businesses and tax-exemption certificates for US organizations. Hetzner and OVHcloud do not describe a self-service exemption on the public pages we could read.
  4. Convert currencies if you are comparing across providers. Hetzner bills in euros; DigitalOcean and OVHcloud bill in dollars. The exchange rate on the day your card is charged, not the day you read the page, decides the real comparison. See our full VPS price breakdown for the pre-tax figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the advertised VPS price tax-inclusive in the US store?

No, at all three providers the advertised VPS price is pre-tax. DigitalOcean states it is required by law to apply taxes for certain countries depending on the team's tax location, and its US tax page lists state and city sales tax rates from 4% to 11.5% that are added to the invoice. OVHcloud's VPS page wraps every price in an HTML element whose class name is tax-excluded-price. Hetzner prints the words excl. VAT next to every monthly and hourly price on its cloud page. None of the three shows a tax-inclusive figure on the pricing page you read before signing up.

What tax gets added at checkout and how is it calculated?

DigitalOcean calculates tax from the account address, which is initially set from the primary payment method's billing address. For US customers it charges state and city sales tax in about two dozen states and cities, at rates ranging from 1% in Connecticut to 11.5% in Puerto Rico, with local surcharges on top. The formula is: advertised price times one plus the state rate plus the local rate. OVHcloud's checkout page requires an account and was not accessible on 2026-10-29, so the exact tax line at checkout could not be read. Hetzner's cloud page has a country selector labeled Country (VAT) but does not display the rate percentage on the page we could read.

How much more does a 4 GB / 2 vCPU VPS cost after tax?

On 2026-10-29 the pre-tax prices for a 4 GB / 2 vCPU VPS were $4.54/month at OVHcloud (VPS-1), $24.00/month at DigitalOcean (Basic), and 5.99 euros per month excl. VAT at Hetzner (CX23). After US sales tax at DigitalOcean the figure rises to $25.44 in Washington D.C. (6%), $25.50 in Texas (state rate 6.25%), or $25.56 in Washington state (state rate 6.5%), calculated as advertised price times one plus the state rate. OVHcloud's checkout was not accessible, so its after-tax figure could not be read. Hetzner's VAT rate is not stated on the page we could read, so the after-tax euro amount could not be calculated.

Can I remove sales tax from my VPS bill?

At DigitalOcean, registered businesses in certain locations can enter a VAT or GST ID to remove those taxes from their monthly bills, and US tax-exempt organizations can submit an exemption certificate via a third party called Ryan ControlPoint. The tax is otherwise mandatory in the locations listed. Hetzner's page does not describe a self-service VAT-removal process on the pages we could read. OVHcloud's tax-removal process was not visible on the public VPS page or the checkout page we could access.

Bottom Line

The VPS price you see is not the VPS price you pay. DigitalOcean adds state sales tax that can push a $24 plan to $26.76 in Puerto Rico; OVHcloud labels every price as tax-excluded in its HTML but does not tell you what tax appears at checkout; Hetzner says “excl. VAT” on every line but hides the rate behind a country selector. The 5.3x gap between a $4.54 OVHcloud VPS and a $24.00 DigitalOcean VPS is a pre-tax gap; after DigitalOcean's tax alone it is 5.6x in Texas, and the OVHcloud side is still unknown.
