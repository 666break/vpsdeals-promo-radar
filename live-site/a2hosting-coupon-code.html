Can two codes be stacked at checkout?

Official pages reviewed 2026-10-19 · hosting.com

Generally, no. hosting.com's plan page already shows a promotional rate that applies at order, and the checkout has a single promo-code field — one code at a time, not two. A code you enter there usually represents an alternative discount rather than an extra layer on top of the already-discounted plan price, so it typically doesn't "add on." The only proof is your own cart total: if entering a code doesn't lower the price you already see, it isn't stacking. Coupon sites list codes and a headline % off, but they almost never say whether a code combines with hosting.com's own promotion — that silence is the gap this page fills.

Does the discount apply to monthly billing, or only to prepaid terms?

Official pages reviewed 2026-10-20 · hosting.com

Usually not the same as on a longer term. hosting.com's own renewal page states the savings come "when paying for a year or two or three in advance" — so the headline discount is generally tied to a prepaid annual or multi-year term, not month-to-month. Monthly billing is available (the site states "no lock-ins or minimum terms"), but it typically forgoes those advance-payment savings and renews at the regular rate after the period. Frequently asked questions

Is there an A2 Hosting coupon code?

No. hosting.com (formerly A2 Hosting) shows no public alphanumeric code; the discount is applied automatically at order, and the checkout has one promo field.

How much does the A2 Hosting discount actually save?

On the Starter shared plan the 12-month first term is $2.99/mo ($35.88/yr, promo WH1YAL auto-applied) against a $13.99/mo standard rate — the discount is real, but it is a first-term benefit.

What happens at renewal?

The rate resets to the standard price: hosting.com’s renewal page says plans renew at regular pricing after the prepaid period ends. On Starter that is $13.99/mo against the $2.99/mo intro rate, about 4.7×.

Does the discount work on monthly billing?

No — the savings are tied to prepaying a year or more. Month-to-month Starter is $13.99/mo with no discount.

Can I stack two codes?

Generally no. There is a single promo field, and the plan-page promo is already the baseline the cart starts from, so a code usually replaces rather than adds.

Is there a student or .edu discount?

No student-, education- or .edu-specific programme is published on hosting.com’s official pages as of 2026-10-24; students get the same auto-applied promo plus the freebies.

Is the free domain really free?

It is a waived registration fee on eligible plans, not a domain you own free forever — hosting.com publishes its own domain price list ($10.99 and $11.99 appear there) and the domain and hosting terms run on different clocks.

Is Black Friday cheaper than the everyday promo?

Usually not by much: the everyday rate is already a promotional rate, so a Black Friday banner mostly re-labels the same discount. Compare the absolute checkout total, not the % off.
