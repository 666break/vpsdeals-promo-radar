How the unit costs are calculated

  • $/GB RAM = monthly price ÷ RAM in GB.
  • $/vCPU = monthly price ÷ vCPU count.
  • $/GB storage = monthly price ÷ included NVMe/SSD in GB.

Nothing is weighted, averaged or normalised beyond that division. Each unit cost is printed as the exact quotient wherever it terminates — $1.135 is 4.54 ÷ 4, $0.97375 is 23.37 ÷ 24 — and rounded half-up to four decimals only where it never terminates. The spreads are computed from the unrounded values.

One caveat that changes the ranking

The three providers are not billed the same way, and the gap widens as you climb the tiers. OVHcloud's figures are 12-month-upfront rates on its own configurator, ScalaHosting's are 12-month-prepaid rates that renew higher, and DigitalOcean bills per second. A headline that is cheap because it locks in a year is not the same offer as one you can leave next month — that is why the billing-term row and the renewal gap sit in the tables above rather than in a footnote.
