Frequently asked questions

Is there an official HostGator coupon code right now?

No alphanumeric promo code is currently displayed on HostGator's official hosting page. The plan-level discounts (Hatchling 66%, Baby 73%, Business 72%, Pro 53%) are auto-applied on a 36-month term at checkout.

How do I get the cheapest HostGator plan?

Pick the Baby shared plan on a 36-month term on HostGator's official order page. The 73% discount is applied automatically.

How much can I save with HostGator?

Baby at 73% off is the largest plan discount, followed by Business at 72% and Hatchling at 66%. All require a 36-month term and renew at the standard rate. Free domain for 1 year on 12/24/36-month plans, Pro Email 1-month trial, and a free site migration tool are bundled.

Does HostGator offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. HostGator offers a 30-day money-back guarantee that refunds hosting fees in full during the first 30 calendar days. No refunds are given after 30 calendar days.

Are HostGator domains refundable?

Free domain registrations are not refundable as a promotional service: if you cancel hosting within the first year, a non-refundable $17.99 domain fee applies. Newly registered domains cannot be transferred to another registrar during the first 60 days.
