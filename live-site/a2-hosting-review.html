Company background (verified)

hosting.com's about page lays out the lineage plainly: A2 Hosting was founded in Ann Arbor, Michigan, built a reputation for speed and real support, and expanded globally (Kenya Web Experts and EYHost are named as part of that story). It is now described as "the best of World Host Group and A2 Hosting under one global brand." The page also states the operation is run by 800+ real people and offers 24/7 support. A later acquisition of Rocket.net strengthened its managed WordPress position. In short: this is the same US-based A2 Hosting business, rebranded — not a different or drop-shipped host.

VPS infrastructure (verified / corroborated)

Across hosting.com product materials and corroborating industry listings, the VPS platform is built on AMD EPYC processors with Samsung NVMe storage, carries a 99.9% uptime SLA, and is supported by 24/7/365 in-house teams (the company emphasises "no outsourcing, no bots"). Unmanaged VPS gives full root access with a choice of Ubuntu, Debian, or AlmaLinux and optional cPanel; managed options add server care on top. Locations are listed as 10 global data centers with a free CDN, serving customers in 180+ countries. Because hardware and exact node specs can change, confirm the current configuration on the official plan page before you buy.

We do not publish a single A2/hosting.com VPS price because the plans are configured per OS, resource tier, and billing term rather than sold at one standing rate. If you want a hard number, the official configurator is the source of truth; our verified comparison shows where it lands against providers with flat published pricing.

The money-back catch (read this before buying)

hosting.com advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee on eligible hosting services — but, consistent across its terms and multiple third-party reviews, VPS plans are excluded, and the guarantee does not apply to renewal charges either. That matters: a VPS is effectively a commit, not a trial you can bail on after three weeks. If you need a refund safety net, test the server hard in the first days (deploy, benchmark, break things on purpose) and budget as if the charge is final.

  • 24/7/365 in-house support — explicitly not outsourced.

Cons

Who should pick hosting.com VPS

hosting.com VPS fits developers and technical buyers who want solid AMD EPYC/NVMe hardware, a 99.9% SLA, and genuinely in-house support, and who don't need a refund safety net. If you run your own stack, value root access and a clean Linux image, and want a globally distributed host with a real support bench, it is a credible pick. The 10 data centers also make regional placement easy.
