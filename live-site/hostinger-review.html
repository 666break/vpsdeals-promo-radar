Hostinger Review (2026): VPS Pricing, Pros, Cons & Who It's For

By Marcus Reid · Published Oct 13, 2026 · Last updated Oct 13, 2026 · 9 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Hostinger is a large, independent hosting company founded in 2004 in Kaunas, Lithuania, now headquartered in Vilnius, serving 5M+ customers in 150+ countries across 30+ languages.
  • Its cheapest VPS, KVM 1, is $6.49/month on a 2-year prepaid term (regularly $19.49, renewing at $11.99): 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe, 4 TB bandwidth. The banner advertises up to 70% off VPS.
  • Biggest strength: beginner-friendliness — AI site builder, in-house hPanel, 24/7 support in 30+ languages, and a 30-day money-back guarantee on hosting.
  • Two honest catches: the promo price requires a multi-year prepaid commitment and renews higher, and VPS plans are excluded from the 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • Global footprint: data centers in Europe, North America, Asia, and South America, plus CDN edges in Japan, Sydney, and Johannesburg.

Hostinger shows up constantly in "cheap hosting" search results, and for good reason — its advertised prices are among the lowest. But low sticker price and "right for you" are different questions. This review separates the verified facts (traced to hostinger.com, reviewed 2026-10-13) from the marketing, and tells you who actually benefits from a Hostinger VPS versus who should look elsewhere.

Company background (verified)

Hostinger began in November 2004 in Kaunas, Lithuania, as "Hosting Media", launched the free-hosting brand 000webhost in 2007, and introduced the Hostinger brand with its in-house hPanel control panel in 2011. It is a private company headquartered in Vilnius, never publicly listed, with a minority stake held by a private-equity firm. As of its official about page, it serves 5M+ customers across 150+ countries and employs 1000+ people. That scale matters: it is not a fly-by-night coupon-site brand, and it owns and operates its own infrastructure (LiteSpeed servers, NVMe storage, a global CDN).

Hostinger VPS pricing (verified)

VPS plans are paid upfront. The entry plan's advertised price is a promo rate that only applies on a 2-year prepaid term; the renewal rate is higher. Here is the cheapest tier exactly as stated on the official VPS page:

The VPS page advertises "up to 70% off" overall, with the KVM 1 plan specifically shown at about 67% off the regular rate. The discount only holds on the long prepaid term; budget for the $11.99/month renewal from day one.

Where Hostinger runs (verified data centers)

Hostinger lists data centers in Europe (France, Germany, Lithuania, Netherlands, United Kingdom), North America (Phoenix, Boston, Asheville), Asia (India, Indonesia, Malaysia), and South America (Brazil). Its CDN adds coverage in Japan, Sydney, and Johannesburg. All data-center electricity is matched with 100% renewable energy. For a VPS, region choice drives latency, so pick the location closest to your users.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very low entry price on long prepaid terms (KVM 1 at $6.49/mo).
  • Beginner-focused: AI site builder, hPanel, 24/7 support in 30+ languages.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee on hosting plans.
  • Large, stable, privately-owned company (founded 2004, 5M+ customers).
  • Broad global data-center footprint across four continents.
  • Self-owned infrastructure and global CDN keep costs down.

Cons

  • Promo price requires a multi-year prepaid commitment.
  • Renewal is at a higher rate ($11.99/mo for KVM 1).
  • VPS plans are excluded from the 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • Unmanaged VPS means you harden and maintain the server yourself.
  • Resource ceilings are modest at the cheapest tier (1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM).
  • Best value is locked behind the longest prepaid term, not month-to-month.

Who should pick Hostinger VPS

Hostinger VPS fits beginners and small projects that want a low upfront price and don't mind committing to a 2-year term: a personal site, a small API, a learning box, or a low-traffic client project. The hPanel and 24/7 support lower the learning curve, and the global data centers make it easy to place the server near your users. If you are comfortable managing an unmanaged Linux server and want the cheapest credible entry point, KVM 1 at $6.49/month is hard to beat on paper.

Who should look elsewhere

Skip Hostinger VPS if you need month-to-month flexibility — the real price is the renewal rate once the promo ends, and the long prepaid term is what unlocks the discount. Also skip it if you expect to rely on a refund: VPS plans are excluded from the 30-day money-back guarantee. For heavier or production workloads, the 1 vCPU / 4 GB ceiling at the cheapest tier will feel tight quickly, and you would compare better against providers like DigitalOcean (transparent per-second billing, Basic Droplet from $4.00/mo) or Contabo (Cloud VPS 4 at $5.28/mo for 4 vCPU / 8 GB).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is Hostinger VPS in 2026?

The cheapest VPS plan, KVM 1, is advertised at $6.49/month on a 2-year prepaid term (regularly $19.49/month, renewing at $11.99/month). It includes 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe SSD, and 4 TB bandwidth. Longer prepaid terms unlock the largest discounts, and renewal is at the higher standard rate.

Is Hostinger good for beginners?

Hostinger positions itself heavily for beginners: an AI site builder, the in-house hPanel control panel, 24/7 support in 30+ languages, and a 30-day money-back guarantee on hosting. VPS plans, however, require you to manage the server yourself and are excluded from the 30-day money-back guarantee.

Where are Hostinger data centers?

Hostinger lists data centers in Europe (France, Germany, Lithuania, Netherlands, United Kingdom), North America (Phoenix, Boston, Asheville), Asia (India, Indonesia, Malaysia), and South America (Brazil), plus CDN coverage in Japan, Sydney, and Johannesburg. It serves customers in 150+ countries.

Should I buy Hostinger VPS on the promo price?

The promo price requires a multi-year prepaid commitment and renews at a higher rate, so only commit if you intend to keep the server past the promo window. Also note VPS plans are excluded from the 30-day money-back guarantee, so test the server thoroughly within the first days and budget for the renewal price from the start.

Bottom Line

Hostinger is a legitimately large, stable, and genuinely cheap hosting company, and its VPS entry point — KVM 1 at $6.49/month on a 2-year term — is among the lowest you will find from a mainstream provider. The trade-offs are predictable and worth naming out loud: the discount is term-locked and renews higher, and VPS plans sit outside the 30-day refund window. If you can commit to the term and you're comfortable running an unmanaged server, it is a strong budget pick. If you need flexibility or a safety net, compare it against DigitalOcean and Contabo first.
